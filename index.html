About ./me

Building autonomous systems that operate in the real world

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student who lives at the intersection of hardware and software — designing drone frames by day and writing ROS 2 nodes by night. At IIT Hyderabad I worked on stereo vision pipelines for depth estimation and obstacle detection on Raspberry Pi and Hailo-8 accelerators. At the AIM Lab, IIT Bhubaneswar, I redesigned a 14-DOF semi-biped into a 16-DOF humanoid, trained SAC/TD3/PPO policies for stable walking, and built a multi-terrain bio-inspired snake robot.

I believe in India's vision of technological self-reliance by 2047, contributing through open research, alumni-funded UAV projects, and building the next generation of robotics engineers as President of YugTroniX.

Biped Humanoid & Snake Robot — IIT Bhubaneswar
AIM Lab · Under Dr. V. Pandu Ranga

Built the vision stack (face/gesture/pose recognition, YOLOv8 tracking) with TF-mini LiDAR obstacle avoidance and potential-field navigation. Deployed on Raspberry Pi + Hailo-8 for real-time edge inference, and built ArUco multi-robot tracking for outdoor, GPS-denied terrain.
